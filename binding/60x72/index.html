How much binding for a 60 × 72 quilt?

Throw size quilt, finished 60″ × 72″.

Buy
5/8 yd
Cut 8 strips 2½″ wide across the width of fabric for 276″ of double-fold binding. Metric: 0.6 m.

How it's worked out

  1. Perimeter: (60 + 72) × 2 = 264″.
  2. Add about 12″ to join the ends and turn the corners: 276″.
  3. Each strip across 42″–44″ fabric gives about 40″ usable, minus ~2½″ for each diagonal join → 37½″ per strip.
  4. 276 ÷ 37½ = 7.36 → 8 strips. 8 × 2½″ = 20″ → round up to 5/8 yd.

Bias binding is only needed for curved or scalloped edges; straight-grain strips are fine for square corners.

Same quilt: backing

Backing for a 60 × 72 quilt: 4 1/2 yd of 42″ fabric (2 panels × 80″ with 1 vertical seam).
