How much binding for a 120 × 120 quilt?

King size quilt, finished 120″ × 120″.

Buy
1 yd
Cut 14 strips 2½″ wide across the width of fabric for 492″ of double-fold binding. Metric: 0.9 m.

How it's worked out

  1. Perimeter: (120 + 120) × 2 = 480″.
  2. Add about 12″ to join the ends and turn the corners: 492″.
  3. Each strip across 42″–44″ fabric gives about 40″ usable, minus ~2½″ for each diagonal join → 37½″ per strip.
  4. 492 ÷ 37½ = 13.12 → 14 strips. 14 × 2½″ = 35″ → round up to 1 yd.

Bias binding is only needed for curved or scalloped edges; straight-grain strips are fine for square corners.

Same quilt: backing

Backing for a 120 × 120 quilt: 14 1/4 yd of 42″ fabric (4 panels × 128″ with 3 vertical seams).
