How much binding for a 60 × 60 quilt?

Throw size quilt, finished 60″ × 60″.

Buy
1/2 yd
Cut 7 strips 2½″ wide across the width of fabric for 252″ of double-fold binding. Metric: 0.5 m.

How it's worked out

  1. Perimeter: (60 + 60) × 2 = 240″.
  2. Add about 12″ to join the ends and turn the corners: 252″.
  3. Each strip across 42″–44″ fabric gives about 40″ usable, minus ~2½″ for each diagonal join → 37½″ per strip.
  4. 252 ÷ 37½ = 6.72 → 7 strips. 7 × 2½″ = 17 1/2″ → round up to 1/2 yd.

Bias binding is only needed for curved or scalloped edges; straight-grain strips are fine for square corners.

Same quilt: backing

Backing for a 60 × 60 quilt: 3 7/8 yd of 42″ fabric (2 panels × 68″ with 1 vertical seam).
