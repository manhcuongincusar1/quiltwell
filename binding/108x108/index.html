How much binding for a 108 × 108 quilt?

King size quilt, finished 108″ × 108″.

Buy
7/8 yd
Cut 12 strips 2½″ wide across the width of fabric for 444″ of double-fold binding. Metric: 0.8 m.

How it's worked out

  1. Perimeter: (108 + 108) × 2 = 432″.
  2. Add about 12″ to join the ends and turn the corners: 444″.
  3. Each strip across 42″–44″ fabric gives about 40″ usable, minus ~2½″ for each diagonal join → 37½″ per strip.
  4. 444 ÷ 37½ = 11.84 → 12 strips. 12 × 2½″ = 30″ → round up to 7/8 yd.
