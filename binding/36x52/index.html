How much binding for a 36 × 52 quilt?

Baby size quilt, finished 36″ × 52″.

Buy
1/2 yd
Cut 6 strips 2½″ wide across the width of fabric for 188″ of double-fold binding. Metric: 0.4 m.

How it's worked out

  1. Perimeter: (36 + 52) × 2 = 176″.
  2. Add about 12″ to join the ends and turn the corners: 188″.
  3. Each strip across 42″–44″ fabric gives about 40″ usable, minus ~2½″ for each diagonal join → 37½″ per strip.
  4. 188 ÷ 37½ = 5.01 → 6 strips. 6 × 2½″ = 15″ → round up to 1/2 yd.

Bias binding is only needed for curved or scalloped edges; straight-grain strips are fine for square corners.

Same quilt: backing

Backing for a 36 × 52 quilt: 2 1/2 yd of 42″ fabric (2 panels × 44″ with 1 horizontal seam).
