How much binding for a 72 × 90 quilt?

Twin size quilt, finished 72″ × 90″.

Buy
5/8 yd
Cut 9 strips 2½″ wide across the width of fabric for 336″ of double-fold binding. Metric: 0.6 m.

How it's worked out

  1. Perimeter: (72 + 90) × 2 = 324″.
  2. Add about 12″ to join the ends and turn the corners: 336″.
  3. Each strip across 42″–44″ fabric gives about 40″ usable, minus ~2½″ for each diagonal join → 37½″ per strip.
  4. 336 ÷ 37½ = 8.96 → 9 strips. 9 × 2½″ = 22 1/2″ → round up to 5/8 yd.

Bias binding is only needed for curved or scalloped edges; straight-grain strips are fine for square corners.

Same quilt: backing

Backing for a 72 × 90 quilt: 6 3/4 yd of 42″ fabric (3 panels × 80″ with 2 horizontal seams).
