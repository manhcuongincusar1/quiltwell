Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-13

Quiltwell does not collect personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics SDKs and no advertising SDKs. Your measurements and saved projects are stored only on your device (and in your iCloud or Google device backup, if you use one).

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive no payment details; the store tells the app only whether you have an active Pro purchase.

Website. This website stores your unit preference (inches or centimetres) in your browser. Our host may keep standard server logs (IP address, pages requested) for security. Some links may be affiliate links; the retailer may set its own cookies when you click them.

Contact: support@konginsight.com
