Common questions

How do I restore Quiltwell Pro on a new device?

Open Quiltwell → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account (or Google account on Android) you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone/iPad: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Quiltwell. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. The lifetime option never renews.

Why does Quiltwell round up?

Fabric is sold in ⅛-yard steps (or 10 cm), so every amount is rounded up to what you can actually buy — you never come home short.

My fabric is wider or narrower than 42″

Change Usable fabric width in Settings; strips and yardage update everywhere.

Free quilting calculators by Quiltwell. Amounts round up to the next ⅛ yard; always double-check before you cut.
