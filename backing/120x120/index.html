How much backing fabric for a 120 × 120 quilt?

King size quilt, finished 120″ × 120″ (304.8 cm × 304.8 cm).

Buy
14 1/4 yd
of standard 42″–44″ quilting cotton — 4 panels × 128″ with 3 vertical seams. Or 7 1/8 yd of 108″ wide backing (2 panels × 128″ with 1 vertical seam). Metric: 13.1 m of 110 cm fabric.

How it's worked out

  1. Add 4″ on every side for quilting (long-arm quilters usually ask for 4″–5″): 120 + 8 = 128″ wide, 120 + 8 = 128″ long.
  2. Quilting cotton is 42″–44″ wide; after trimming selvages about 40″ is usable, and each seam takes 1″.
  3. Running the seams vertically you need 4 widths of fabric, each 128″ long.
  4. 4 × 128″ = 512″ = 14.22 yards → round up to the next ⅛ yard: 14 1/4 yd.

Sewing the seams the other way (horizontal) would take 4 panels × 128″ with 3 horizontal seams = 14 1/4 yd, so vertical seams save fabric.

Same quilt: binding & batting

  • Binding for a 120 × 120 quilt: 14 strips of 2½″ → 1 yd.
  • Batting: at least 128″ × 128″ — buy it by the yard.
