How much backing fabric for a 80 × 90 quilt?

Full size quilt, finished 80″ × 90″ (203.2 cm × 228.6 cm).

Buy
7 3/8 yd
of standard 42″–44″ quilting cotton — 3 panels × 88″ with 2 horizontal seams. Or 2 1/2 yd of 108″ wide backing (one piece, no seams — cut 88″). Metric: 6.8 m of 110 cm fabric.

How it's worked out

  1. Add 4″ on every side for quilting (long-arm quilters usually ask for 4″–5″): 80 + 8 = 88″ wide, 90 + 8 = 98″ long.
  2. Quilting cotton is 42″–44″ wide; after trimming selvages about 40″ is usable, and each seam takes 1″.
  3. Running the seams horizontally you need 3 widths of fabric, each 88″ long.
  4. 3 × 88″ = 264″ = 7.33 yards → round up to the next ⅛ yard: 7 3/8 yd.

Sewing the seams the other way (vertical) would take 3 panels × 98″ with 2 vertical seams = 8 1/4 yd, so horizontal seams save fabric.

Same quilt: binding & batting

  • Binding for a 80 × 90 quilt: 10 strips of 2½″ → 3/4 yd.
  • Batting: at least 88″ × 98″ — a Queen package fits, or 2 3/4 yd off a 96″ roll.
