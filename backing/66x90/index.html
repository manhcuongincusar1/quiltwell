How much backing fabric for a 66 × 90 quilt?

Twin size quilt, finished 66″ × 90″ (167.6 cm × 228.6 cm).

Buy
5 1/2 yd
of standard 42″–44″ quilting cotton — 2 panels × 98″ with 1 vertical seam. Or 2 1/8 yd of 108″ wide backing (one piece, no seams — cut 74″). Metric: 5.0 m of 110 cm fabric.

How it's worked out

  1. Add 4″ on every side for quilting (long-arm quilters usually ask for 4″–5″): 66 + 8 = 74″ wide, 90 + 8 = 98″ long.
  2. Quilting cotton is 42″–44″ wide; after trimming selvages about 40″ is usable, and each seam takes 1″.
  3. Running the seams vertically you need 2 widths of fabric, each 98″ long.
  4. 2 × 98″ = 196″ = 5.44 yards → round up to the next ⅛ yard: 5 1/2 yd.

Sewing the seams the other way (horizontal) would take 3 panels × 74″ with 2 horizontal seams = 6 1/4 yd, so vertical seams save fabric.

Same quilt: binding & batting

  • Binding for a 66 × 90 quilt: 9 strips of 2½″ → 5/8 yd.
  • Batting: at least 74″ × 98″ — a Queen package fits, or 2 3/4 yd off a 96″ roll.
