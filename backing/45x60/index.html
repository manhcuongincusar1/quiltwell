How much backing fabric for a 45 × 60 quilt?

Crib size quilt, finished 45″ × 60″ (114.3 cm × 152.4 cm).

Buy
3 yd
of standard 42″–44″ quilting cotton — 2 panels × 53″ with 1 horizontal seam. Or 1 1/2 yd of 108″ wide backing (one piece, no seams — cut 53″). Metric: 2.7 m of 110 cm fabric.

How it's worked out

  1. Add 4″ on every side for quilting (long-arm quilters usually ask for 4″–5″): 45 + 8 = 53″ wide, 60 + 8 = 68″ long.
  2. Quilting cotton is 42″–44″ wide; after trimming selvages about 40″ is usable, and each seam takes 1″.
  3. Running the seams horizontally you need 2 widths of fabric, each 53″ long.
  4. 2 × 53″ = 106″ = 2.94 yards → round up to the next ⅛ yard: 3 yd.

Sewing the seams the other way (vertical) would take 2 panels × 68″ with 1 vertical seam = 3 7/8 yd, so horizontal seams save fabric.

Same quilt: binding & batting

  • Binding for a 45 × 60 quilt: 6 strips of 2½″ → 1/2 yd.
  • Batting: at least 53″ × 68″ — a Twin package fits, or 1 1/2 yd off a 96″ roll.
