How much backing fabric for a 108 × 108 quilt?

King size quilt, finished 108″ × 108″ (274.3 cm × 274.3 cm).

Buy
9 3/4 yd
of standard 42″–44″ quilting cotton — 3 panels × 116″ with 2 vertical seams. Or 6 1/2 yd of 108″ wide backing (2 panels × 116″ with 1 vertical seam). Metric: 8.9 m of 110 cm fabric.

How it's worked out

  1. Add 4″ on every side for quilting (long-arm quilters usually ask for 4″–5″): 108 + 8 = 116″ wide, 108 + 8 = 116″ long.
  2. Quilting cotton is 42″–44″ wide; after trimming selvages about 40″ is usable, and each seam takes 1″.
  3. Running the seams vertically you need 3 widths of fabric, each 116″ long.
  4. 3 × 116″ = 348″ = 9.67 yards → round up to the next ⅛ yard: 9 3/4 yd.

Sewing the seams the other way (horizontal) would take 3 panels × 116″ with 2 horizontal seams = 9 3/4 yd, so vertical seams save fabric.
