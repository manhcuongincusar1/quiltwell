How much backing fabric for a 86 × 100 quilt?

Full size quilt, finished 86″ × 100″ (218.4 cm × 254 cm).

Buy
7 7/8 yd
of standard 42″–44″ quilting cotton — 3 panels × 94″ with 2 horizontal seams. Or 3 yd of 108″ wide backing (one piece, no seams — cut 108″). Metric: 7.2 m of 110 cm fabric.

How it's worked out

  1. Add 4″ on every side for quilting (long-arm quilters usually ask for 4″–5″): 86 + 8 = 94″ wide, 100 + 8 = 108″ long.
  2. Quilting cotton is 42″–44″ wide; after trimming selvages about 40″ is usable, and each seam takes 1″.
  3. Running the seams horizontally you need 3 widths of fabric, each 94″ long.
  4. 3 × 94″ = 282″ = 7.83 yards → round up to the next ⅛ yard: 7 7/8 yd.

Sewing the seams the other way (vertical) would take 3 panels × 108″ with 2 vertical seams = 9 yd, so horizontal seams save fabric.

Same quilt: binding & batting

  • Binding for a 86 × 100 quilt: 11 strips of 2½″ → 7/8 yd.
  • Batting: at least 94″ × 108″ — a King package fits, or 3 yd off a 96″ roll.
