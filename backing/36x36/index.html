How much backing fabric for a 36 × 36 quilt?

Baby size quilt, finished 36″ × 36″ (91.4 cm × 91.4 cm).

Buy
2 1/2 yd
of standard 42″–44″ quilting cotton — 2 panels × 44″ with 1 vertical seam. Or 1 1/4 yd of 108″ wide backing (one piece, no seams — cut 44″). Metric: 2.3 m of 110 cm fabric.

How it's worked out

  1. Add 4″ on every side for quilting (long-arm quilters usually ask for 4″–5″): 36 + 8 = 44″ wide, 36 + 8 = 44″ long.
  2. Quilting cotton is 42″–44″ wide; after trimming selvages about 40″ is usable, and each seam takes 1″.
  3. Running the seams vertically you need 2 widths of fabric, each 44″ long.
  4. 2 × 44″ = 88″ = 2.44 yards → round up to the next ⅛ yard: 2 1/2 yd.

Sewing the seams the other way (horizontal) would take 2 panels × 44″ with 1 horizontal seam = 2 1/2 yd, so vertical seams save fabric.

Same quilt: binding & batting

  • Binding for a 36 × 36 quilt: 5 strips of 2½″ → 3/8 yd.
  • Batting: at least 44″ × 44″ — a Crib package fits, or 1 1/4 yd off a 96″ roll.
