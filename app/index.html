Quiltwell: Quilting Calculator

Every quilt calculation in one place — on iPhone, iPad and Android, even at the fabric shop.

  • Backing, binding & batting with a drawing of your backing panels
  • Borders (butted or mitred), block layouts and on-point setting triangles
  • Cut sizes for half-square triangles, quarter-square triangles and flying geese
  • Cutting yardage for any piece size, fat-quarter yields and pre-cut planner
  • Save projects and share a fabric shopping list
  • Inches with fractions, or centimetres and metres
